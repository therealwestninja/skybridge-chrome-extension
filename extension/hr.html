Rook — Heart Rate

Reads your BLE heart-rate sensor directly over this PC's Bluetooth (standard HR profile 0x180D) and streams it to Rook-enabled Perchance generators via the Skybridge soma capability. No phone, no server.

Keep this tab open while you want live HR (the Bluetooth link lives here). A BLE HR sensor allows one connection at a time, so don't have the phone (Rook-Face) connected to the same sensor simultaneously. In a generator: sb.request('soma',{op:'latest'}) or sb.bus.subscribe('soma:sensors', fn).
