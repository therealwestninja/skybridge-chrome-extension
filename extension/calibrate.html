The FIRE/RELEASE gap is hysteresis — a trigger fires its signal once when it crosses FIRE and won't re-fire until it drops below RELEASE, so a resting-heavy trigger can't chatter. A wider gap = steadier; a narrower gap = more responsive. The deadzone ignores small stick drift near center.
